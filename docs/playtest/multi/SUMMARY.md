# Multi-agent playtest — summary (2026-10-03, UTC)

One full match per game, 3–6 AI players (haiku + sonnet), each driving its own 390×844 phone window through `tools/playtest/pt.mjs`; one opus reviewer per game verified every finding against `docs/research/<id>.md` and the code. Per-game reports: `docs/playtest/multi/<id>.md`. 68 agents, ~3.4 h.

No app blocker survived verification in any game. 8 of 10 matches finished on their own; 9upper ran 3 of 12 rounds (the 官方玩法 preset overrode `laps:1` and AI seats went idle) and custom hung on a native `confirm()` (finding #3).
